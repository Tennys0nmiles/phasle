      How to play

      Each day there is one Shape (4 tries) and one Song (7 tries). Every guess or skip unlocks the next tier. The reconstruction is the only clue you get.

      Shape

      Today's picture is drawn with a single closed line, inner lines included. Any closed line is a Fourier series: a chain of spinning circles, each turning at a whole-number speed, with the pen on the tip of the last one.

      z(t) = Σ ck eikt

      Each tier keeps the biggest circles, as many as it takes to reach that tier's error.

      Song

      The whole clip is one Fourier series: a sum of pure sine waves, each one held for the entire clip. A few hundred give a drone. It takes tens of thousands before the rhythm and the voice come through.

      x(t) = Σ Aj cos(2πfjt + φj)

      % error

      RMS distance between the reconstruction and the real thing at each moment, as a share of the real thing's own size. Drawing nothing (or silence) is 100%. Shape tiers step through 50 → 30 → 10 → 1%, song tiers through 90 → 77 → 63 → 50 → 30 → 10 → 1%.
