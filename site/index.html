      How to play

      Each day there is one Shape and one Song. Name them in 6 tries.

      Shape

      Today's picture is drawn with a single closed line. Any closed line can be written as a Fourier series: a chain of spinning circles, each turning at a whole-number speed, with the pen on the tip of the last one.

      z(t) = Σ ck eikt

      You start with the 2 biggest circles. Every guess or skip adds more, so the curve sharpens.

      Song

      Every moment of a song is a sum of sine waves. At each tier we keep only the N loudest sine waves at each instant and throw the rest away. 2 sines sound like a ghost. 64 sound like the song through a wall.

      % error

      How far the reconstruction is from the real thing: the size of everything we left out, as a share of the whole signal.

      correct close wrong skipped
